About Me

My research interests lie at the intersection of large vision-language model reasoning, multimodal fusion, and multimodal AI safety evaluation. I am particularly interested in how foundation models understand heterogeneous information, perform reliable reasoning, and mitigate potential safety risks.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I worked on multimodal fusion, exploring Transformer-, Mamba-, and Vision State Space Model-based methods for high-resolution remote-sensing image generation. These experiences motivate my current research on multimodal foundation models, intelligent reasoning, and AI safety.
